Engineering Report - 150 Ocean Example Terrace, Surfside, FL 30055

Property Overview
The subject property is a two-story wood-framed residence with an asphalt shingle roof and aluminum soffit and fascia at the eaves. The site visit addressed reported wind damage after the hurricane.

Roof Observations
Asphalt shingles on the north-facing slope were missing, creased, and torn. Granule loss was widespread across the field shingles, and several tab edges were lifted. A storm-created opening was confirmed where shingles and underlayment were displaced.

Soffit and fascia along the north eave were detached and bent outward. The damaged soffit created an additional opening through which wind-driven rain entered the roof edge and attic space.

Interior Observations
Attic sheathing near the north eave showed fresh water staining. In the upstairs hallway, drywall joint cracking and paint blistering were observed below the affected roof slope.

Conclusion
Wind damage to the shingles, soffit, and fascia created storm-created openings that permitted water entry into the roof assembly and interior. The observed conditions are consistent with a single hurricane event.
