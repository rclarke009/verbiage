Engineering Report - 800 Sand Example Road, Duneville, FL 30060

Property Overview
The subject property is a single-story concrete block residence with a hip roof covered by architectural shingles. The inspection evaluated reported exterior and interior damage following the windstorm.

Roof and Envelope Observations
Shingles along the western hip ridge were torn and missing, exposing the underlayment. Wind uplift displaced flashing at the chimney cricket, forming a storm-created opening at the roof penetration.

The exterior stucco finish on the west elevation exhibited hairline cracking and spalling at the window corners. Wind-driven rain entered through the compromised roof and migrated into the wall cavity, contributing to interior staining around the window openings.

Interior Observations
In the guest bedroom, cracking was observed between the crown molding and the wall. Staining on the adjacent ceiling drywall is consistent with water that entered through the storm-created opening in the roof and traveled down the west wall.

Conclusion
The roof shingle damage, stucco cracking at the window corners, and interior water staining are the result of wind-driven rain entering through storm-created openings. The findings are consistent with the reported windstorm and not with long-term settlement alone.
