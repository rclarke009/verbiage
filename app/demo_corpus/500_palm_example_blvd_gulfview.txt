Engineering Report - 500 Palm Example Boulevard, Gulfview, FL 30050

Property Overview
The subject property is a single-story stucco residence with a barrel-tile roof. The inspection was requested to document hail and wind-related damage following the named windstorm event.

Roof Observations
Multiple concrete roof tiles exhibited circular impact fractures consistent with hailstone strikes. Several tiles along the south slope were cracked through the body of the tile, and displaced fragments were observed in the landscaping below.

Wind-driven rain entered through cracked and displaced tiles, creating a storm-created opening in the roof covering. Underlayment at two locations was exposed and showed recent moisture staining.

Interior Observations
In the living room, ceiling drywall staining was present near the south wall. Moisture readings at the stained area were elevated relative to a dry baseline location in the same room.

Conclusion
The cracked and displaced roof tiles, together with interior ceiling staining, are consistent with hail impact and subsequent water intrusion through storm-created openings in the tile roof. The damage pattern is attributable to the reported windstorm event.
