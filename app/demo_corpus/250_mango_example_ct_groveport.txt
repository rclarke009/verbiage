Engineering Report - 250 Mango Example Court, Groveport, FL 30065

Property Overview
The subject property is a single-story ranch residence with a metal-panel roof over the main structure and a screened lanai. The homeowner reported possible storm damage to the roof and exterior.

Roof Observations
The metal roof panels were intact, properly fastened, and free of dents, punctures, or displacement. No storm-created opening was identified in the roof covering. Fastener washers and sealant were in serviceable condition for the age of the installation.

Exterior Observations
Hairline stucco cracks at the garage door lintel were consistent with thermal movement and normal curing shrinkage rather than impact or wind loading. No fresh spalling, impact marks, or pattern damage attributable to hail was observed.

Interior Observations
No moisture staining, elevated moisture readings, or drywall damage consistent with recent water intrusion were identified in the living areas inspected.

Conclusion
Based on the field observations, no storm-related roof or exterior damage was documented at this residence. The minor stucco cracking is consistent with age and thermal movement and is unrelated to a windstorm event. No storm-created opening was found.
